Services

One pipeline. Every discipline.

From the first cut to the final delivery, all under one roof.

01

Offline Editing

The story found, cut by cut.

Every project starts as hours of raw footage with no shape yet — the offline edit is where that turns into a story. We work through the material scene by scene, testing structure, pace and performance until the cut earns its runtime. It's slower, more deliberate work than people expect: less about assembling clips and more about deciding what the film is actually about. By the time picture is locked, everything downstream has a solid foundation to build on.

02

Online Editing

Conformed and finished, ready for delivery.

Once picture is locked, the online conform takes that offline cut and rebuilds it against the original camera files, frame for frame. Every transition, every retime, every reframe gets matched back to source quality, so nothing that made the cut looks compressed or soft by the time it reaches an audience. It's unglamorous, exacting work, and it's the reason a project can move into grading and sound without anyone finding a mismatch days before delivery.

03

Color Grading / DI

The look, set frame by frame.

Grading is where a project finds its temperature — warm or cold, saturated or restrained, consistent from the first frame to the last regardless of how many locations, lenses or lighting setups it was shot across. We work shot by shot, balancing skin tones, correcting for whatever went wrong on set, and building toward a look that serves the story rather than showing off. The difference between a graded and ungraded cut is the difference between footage and a finished picture.

04

VFX

Shots that could not be filmed.

Some shots can't be filmed the way they're written — the timing's wrong, the location doesn't exist, or the moment is too dangerous or too expensive to capture for real. That's where VFX comes in: clean-up work that removes what shouldn't be in frame, composites that combine elements shot separately into one believable moment, effects built to sit inside the plate rather than on top of it. Good VFX is invisible — nobody should ever notice it happened.

05

CGI

Whole worlds, built from nothing, photoreal.

When there's nothing to shoot at all — a product that doesn't exist yet, an environment too costly to build, a shot only possible from an angle no camera could reach — we build it from scratch. Full CG assets, environments and objects, lit and rendered to match live footage closely enough that the join disappears. It's slower and more technical than compositing, but it opens up shots that live-action alone simply can't deliver.

06

Flame Finishing

High-end finishing on Autodesk Flame.

Some sequences need one artist to own every shot from start to finish — commercials with heavy compositing, high-end title sequences, anything where consistency shot to shot matters more than turnaround speed. That's what Flame finishing is for: dedicated, shot-by-shot work on Autodesk Flame, the kind of finishing pass that catches what a faster pipeline would miss.

07

Mastering & Final Delivery

Every format, every platform, on spec.

Every platform, broadcaster and territory has its own technical spec — frame rate, loudness, file wrapper, colour space, the works — and a project that doesn't match it doesn't ship. Mastering is the last technical gate before delivery: exporting masters built to each destination's exact requirements, checked against the spec sheet rather than assumed to be close enough.

Need something that doesn’t fit neatly into one of these — a mixed deliverable, an unusual format, a tight turnaround across a few of them at once? That’s most weeks for us. Get in touch and describe the job.
